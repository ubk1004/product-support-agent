## Status

Proposed, accepted, superseded, or deprecated.

## Context

Describe the forces and constraints that require a decision.

## Decision

State the selected approach and its boundaries.

## Rationale

Explain why the selected approach best fits the approved requirements.

## Alternatives considered

List realistic alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Consequences

Record benefits, costs, risks, and follow-up work.

## References

Link to authoritative documentation and related decisions.
